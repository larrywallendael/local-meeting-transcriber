UI rules:

• #EFE9DC — earth beige
Use as main app background. Warm, soft, and makes the blue pop without contrast fatigue.

• #9DBAE6 — your primary blue
Perfect for cards, highlights, focus states, selections, info blocks.

• #2F2F2F — charcoal
Primary text, icons, headings. Much better than pure black on beige.

• #8B8F94 — muted grey
Secondary text, metadata, disabled states.
